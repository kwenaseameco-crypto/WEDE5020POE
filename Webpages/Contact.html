Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Orders typically ship within 3-5 business days. Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days, while expedited shipping takes 2-3 business days.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes! We ship to most countries worldwide. International shipping rates and times vary by location. Please contact us for specific details.

Can I return or exchange items?

We offer a 30-day return/exchange policy on all items in original condition with tags attached. Please reach out to our team for return instructions.

What about sponsorship opportunities?

We're always interested in collaborating with brands and creators who align with our values. Fill out the sponsorship form and we'll get back to you within 2-3 business days.

Do you offer wholesale/bulk orders?

Yes! For bulk orders, please contact our wholesale team directly at wholesale@misunderstoodworld.com with details about your project.

How can I become an affiliate?

We'd love to work with you! Email us at partnerships@misunderstoodworld.com with your platform/audience details and we'll discuss affiliate opportunities.
